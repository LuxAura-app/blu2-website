Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-29
This is a draft, written to match what's actually collected at checkout today — it hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Treat it as a starting point.
What we collect

When you place an order through our shop, our payment processor (Stripe) collects the following directly from you at checkout:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping address
  • Billing address
  • Payment details (handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card number)
  • Whether you opted in to marketing texts/emails about new drops and shows (a yes/no checkbox at checkout)

We don't collect anything beyond what's needed to fulfill your order and, if you opt in, to send you occasional updates.

How we use it
  • Your name, email, phone, and shipping address are shared with our fulfillment partner (Apliiq) solely to print and ship your order.
  • Your email is used to send order confirmations and updates about your purchase.
  • If you opted in to marketing at checkout, we may email or text you about new drops and shows. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by contacting us at titledtentatively@gmail.com.
  • We keep a record of orders for accounting, customer support, and fraud-prevention purposes.
Payment processing

All payments are processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number — Stripe handles that in accordance with its own security standards and privacy policy.

Sharing

We share order information only with the vendors needed to run the shop: Stripe (payments) and Apliiq (printing and shipping). We don't sell your information to third parties.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or want to know what information we have about you, reach out at titledtentatively@gmail.com.
